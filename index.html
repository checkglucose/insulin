How to Use the Insulin Tracker

  • Date: Select the date of the meal or leave it blank to default to the current date.
  • Meal Type: Choose the type of meal (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner).
  • Fasting Glucose: Enter your fasting glucose value before the meal (mmol/L).
  • Plates: Enter the number of plates for the meal (estimate of carbs).
  • Short-acting Insulin: Enter the amount of short-acting insulin used for the meal (units).
  • Post-meal Glucose: Enter your glucose level after the meal (mmol/L).
  • Long-acting Insulin (Once Daily): Enter the amount of Long-acting insulin that is used once daily.
  • Long Adjustment: This field will automatically adjust based on your fasting glucose for Breakfast.
  • Delete Row: You can delete a specific row by clicking the "Delete" button in the corresponding row.

Note: This tracker helps you keep track of your insulin doses and glucose levels for better diabetes management.
